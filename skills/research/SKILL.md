# Research a question

Define the question and the decision it informs. Prefer official documentation, specifications, source code, and first-party data. Read the relevant source rather than relying on search snippets; distinguish measured facts, source claims, and inference.

Use a background researcher only when delegation is available and authorized and independent work can continue. Otherwise research directly. Do not require a particular browser, MCP server, or subagent tool.

Write a concise findings file in the requested or established notes location, or `.scratch/research/` when appropriate. Include the question, findings with nearby citations, conflicting evidence, open questions, and practical implications. State the date when time-sensitive facts were checked. Link existing artifacts rather than reproducing them.

Stop when evidence answers the decision sufficiently or further progress requires unavailable access. Report that boundary and the artifact path. Research alone does not authorize implementation or publishing findings externally.
